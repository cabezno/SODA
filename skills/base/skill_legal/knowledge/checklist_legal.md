# Legal Review Checklist

- [ ] Document includes effective date and version number
- [ ] Company/entity identified with full legal name
- [ ] Governing law jurisdiction specified
- [ ] Contact information for legal inquiries included
- [ ] User rights section present (if handling personal data)
- [ ] Third-party services/processors listed
- [ ] Cookie notice includes opt-out mechanism
- [ ] Limitation of liability clause present
- [ ] Termination conditions clear for both parties
- [ ] DISCLAIMER: reviewed by qualified attorney before production use
